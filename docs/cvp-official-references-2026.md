# CVP official references (October 2026)

Anthropic's Cyber Verification Program was updated in October 2026. Individuals can apply only for Defense Access and need a paid plan; organizations can apply once through their admin, and approval is discretionary. See https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14604842-cyber-verification-program .

Individual Defense Access requires the identity-provider sign-in and MFA conditions in https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17202708-cyber-verification-program-security-requirements . By December 15, 2026, phishing-resistant MFA and non-static credentials are required. Applicant identity, security work, and required controls must be verified; public commits alone do not guarantee approval.

This document is not proof of program eligibility or an accepted external vulnerability disclosure.
